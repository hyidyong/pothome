# Evidence Rules

Use these rules whenever sources, labels, or public claims disagree.

## Claim contract

Record every candidate claim with:

- exact claim and unit;
- population and denominator;
- source and validity state;
- public approval state;
- outcome/status label;
- known limits.

Omit unknown values. Never infer a duration, team size, denominator, causal result, or organizational adoption.

## Publication rules

1. **Never sum different populations.** Display separate groups separately unless one approved source explicitly defines their aggregation.
2. **Separate raw from valid samples.** Publish the approved valid sample. Keep excluded rows and exclusion details internal unless independently approved.
3. **Separate scope and output from outcomes.** A deck, finding count, interview count, prototype, roadmap, or research model describes work performed; it does not prove adoption, revenue, growth, retention, or impact.
4. **Preserve denominator, status, and limits.** A metric without its population or evidence state is incomplete.
5. **Block conflicting domain copy.** If narrative copy describes a different problem or domain than the verified case, correct the source of truth before publication.
6. **Publish approved aggregates only.** Do not expose personal data, contact details, raw survey or interview rows, personnel records, credentials, or source excerpts that can identify participants.
7. **Gate contact paths.** Do not invent or expose a contact method before the owner approves both the channel and public value.
8. **Prove completion.** Do not claim a portfolio is complete, accessible, responsive, secure, or deployment-ready without current automated checks and browser evidence.

## Metric selection

Choose homepage metrics that are approved, traceable, legible without hidden context, and representative of distinct cases. Keep detailed supporting metrics in case pages with their status and limits. If approval or interpretation remains uncertain, omit the metric and request a decision.
